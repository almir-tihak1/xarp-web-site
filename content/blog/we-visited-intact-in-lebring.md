Between September 15th and 19th, we visited our partner company, Intact GmbH, at their office in Lebring.

After attending the Infobip conference in Zadar last year, where we met in person for the first time, we continued to strengthen our collaboration through ongoing projects, as well as through activities during and after our work.

Besides the opportunity to work together in the office, we had a lot of fun with our Intact colleagues after work.

We visited a chocolate company, Zotter, where everyone had a chance to try some new and interesting chocolate products. The center of Graz was charming and beautiful, and we enjoyed the view from the Schlossberg, but also a little roller coaster ride back, and on the last day, we went on a short hiking tour with minigolf.

We are also glad that Xarp tec was able to participate in the company's Fall Event and meet colleagues from other departments.

Last but not least, we brought a small gift. Souvenir of Sebilj, a symbol of Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Thank you once again for the hospitality, and we are looking forward to our next meetup.
